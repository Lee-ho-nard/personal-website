Languages

I speak German, English, and French, and I'm learning Mandarin — through daily practice with Duolingo, Pleco, and Anki. Four languages isn't just a skill tag for me; it's the same logic as the small businesses: if something's worth having, it's worth building the habit for, one rep at a time.

Training

Outside of building, I stay active through running and the gym — training regularly, five to six times a week, working toward a half-marathon. I also play tennis and padel when I get the chance. None of it is really about the sport itself; it's the same discipline that shows up in building something every day whether or not anyone's watching.
